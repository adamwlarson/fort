# Fort 15 — The Castle Architect

## Plan and see what you are building

Press **K beside a castle sign**. The architect now uses an overview camera and three modes: **Expand**, **Remodel**, and **Dismantle**. Closing the menu returns to your previous camera. The crew and raid keep playing while you plan.

Expansion previews show a 20m × 20m footprint, four gold entrance clearances, stair direction/landings, service or tower space, and support columns for upper floors. Green means valid; red comes with a reason in the menu. The menu lists costs, storey and support requirements. Existing room/height/hearth limits remain.

Project signs now show delivered versus required supplies, remaining materials, and a progress meter that changes from funding to hands-on construction. The architect shows the actual project target, current work, donations and eventual recovery.

## Remodel a completed room

1. Stand beside its sign, press K and select **Remodel**.
2. Choose a different room purpose. The menu shows its full construction cost and the old room's eventual refund.
3. Confirm the project twice, donate materials and hold **E** at the sign to build. Other dwarves can work alongside you.
4. When work finishes, the room changes purpose and half the old room's paid materials return to the shared stockpile, rounded down.

The old floor remains until completion. Cancellation preserves the old room and returns only the unused newly donated materials; it never credits the old-room refund. Learned research stays with the expedition even if its hall later changes.

## Dismantle and reshape

Choose **Dismantle** and confirm twice. No new materials are required, but dwarves must perform demolition work at the sign. Completion removes the room and refunds half its paid room materials. Workers at the sign are returned safely to the keep instead of falling with the floor.

Safety rules are enforced by the host when the project starts AND during work:

- Daylight only, with no enemies within 24m of the room center.
- The central keep cannot be changed or removed.
- Salvage the room's placed defenses and curtain walls separately first. Castle refunds exclude those items. The terrace's included watchtower has its own allocated salvage ledger, deducted from the room refund calculation.
- Other dwarves must leave the room or join the workers at its sign; mounted or downed guests must be cleared.
- Remove upper rooms before dismantling a supporting floor or changing stairs beneath them.
- A room cannot be removed if it would disconnect another wing. Unfinished blueprints cannot serve as access bridges.
- Four ground wings must remain while upper storeys exist.
- New defenses cannot be placed inside an active remodeling/dismantling project. Building footprints now reserve room entrances as well as stairwells and landings.

If a safety condition changes, work pauses without losing donations. Clear the obstruction, wait until daylight, or cancel the project. This is fixed-module remodeling, not free-form walls, movable rooms, or automatic relocation of built towers.

## Saves and multiplayer

Fort 14 saves still load. Legacy completed rooms use their original recipe as their refund ledger. New saves use **format 2** to preserve remodeling targets, paid ledgers and partial work; **Fort 14 cannot read these newer saves**. Back up your `expeditions` folder before switching versions if you want to return to Fort 14.

Everyone should run Fort 15. Room changes, work, funding, refunds and safe worker relocation are host-authoritative and replicate to the crew. Stale/duplicate requests do not repeat charges or refunds. Existing dawn/manual/exit saves remain available.
