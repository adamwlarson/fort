# Fort 5 — Beyond the Hearth

Extract the entire Fort_5 folder and run Fort.exe. All players need this version. Earlier version folders are unchanged. Use the same host IP and UDP port workflow as Fort 4; firewall permission may need to cover this new executable path.

- Hearth upgrades expand exploration to 153m then 243m, and construction to 65m then 115m.
- Rustscar iron and Stormglass aether unlock metal walls, chain-lightning Storm Spires, and slowing Frost Mortars. Seven construction recipes: B, then 1–7.
- Press G beside a permanent defense to upgrade it to levels two and three. Subtle nearby health bars replace building text clutter.
- Crossbows and ballistae have projected aim cursors and real vertical targeting. Hold right mouse to aim the crossbow while walking.
- Craft the Trail Pack and Expedition Frame in the workshop's Backpacks and Relics tab.
- Three supply caches and three enemy camps contain shared treasure. Chieftains guard rare hammers, crossbows and armor; rewards apply to the whole crew and late joiners. Chest locks cost 2 shared crystals.
- Ten nights instead of five. Hearth upgrades add only 5% enemy health and four active slots per tier, with no extra spawn-group enemies. Raids approach from beyond the expanded build boundary.
- Twelve new Blender-made assets, zone-specific terrain and foliage, and new lightning/frost effects.

Controls: U hearth, G defense upgrades, E workshop/chest/deposit/gather, B build, 1–7 recipe, Q rotate, RMB crossbow aim, LMB attack, C switch weapon, R repair, F ability, T travel gear.

All progression lasts for the current session; there is no save system or host migration. Local four-process tests cover progression, concurrent crafting/upgrading, late joining, weapons, and lobby behavior. Rendered checks cover UI, models, aiming, effects and a staged 100-raider scene. Real two-PC LAN/WAN testing and extended co-op balance/performance testing remain necessary.
