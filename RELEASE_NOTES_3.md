# Fort 3 — The Weapon Forge

## New in this version

- A functional weapon forge at the workshop, using deposited shared supplies.
- Hearthbreaker hammer: 14 wood, 18 stone, 2 crystal. Slow heavy strikes, crowd knockback, brief stun.
- Trailguard crossbow: 22 wood, 8 stone, 4 crystal. Aimed ranged shots with reload motion and reusable ammunition. Obstacles block shots.
- New Blender weapon models attached to the animated dwarf's hand.
- Automatic axe/tool swapping when gathering and repairing.
- Multiplayer equipment synchronization, late-join restoration, duplicate-craft protection, and gear retained through rescue.

## Try it

1. Gather wood, stone, and crystal; hold E at the STOCKPILE to deposit.
2. Walk to the WORKSHOP on the other side of the campfire and press E.
3. Choose Craft & Equip. Costs come from shared supplies; each dwarf crafts their own gear.
4. Close with Esc. Press C to cycle owned weapons; hold left-click to attack.

All classes can use every weapon. Engineer discounts apply to defenses, not weapon recipes. The axe remains free and available. The workshop does not pause the world.

This is a development build. Sessions are not saved. Use the same version on all four PCs. Local four-process tests are included; real internet latency and extended balance still need playtesting.
