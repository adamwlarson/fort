# Fort 16 — Read the battlefield

## Castle signs: how K works

1. Press K beside a completed room's sign. Choose an expansion direction and room purpose, then place the blueprint.
2. Visit the NEW project's sign. Press K and add supplies from the shared stockpile, or bring materials yourself.
3. Close the menu and HOLD E beside that sign until construction reaches 100%. Multiple dwarves can help.
4. Reopen K at a completed room to add its walls or expand again. To keep growing outward, use signs on completed outer rooms rather than only the original keep sign.

A sign supports one active project at a time. Funding it alone does not complete it. Finish or cancel its current project before selecting another. Higher-tier rooms require hearth upgrades; upper storeys require the existing completed-wing and stairwell prerequisites. Resource nodes and other obstacles must be cleared from a proposed footprint.

The architect now shows a persistent supply/build/locked explanation, direction readiness, and hearth-tier labels. This pass clarifies existing construction rules; it does not remove resource, clearance, support or progression requirements.

## Clear resources and rewards

- The shared-stock HUD has five distinct resource icons: logs, stone, crystal, iron ingots and aether. Icons match the nearby resource badges and reward receipts.
- Nearby harvestable nodes show their resource, remaining amount, and axe upgrade requirement when locked. Labels are distance-limited and decluttered. Architect mode also labels resources around the preview footprint.
- Harvesting sends a floating icon and actual quantity up from the resource. YOUR PACK, CREW PACK, PET CARGO and SHARED STOCK distinguish where the materials went. Harvested personal supplies still need depositing at the fort.
- Treasure and caravan receipts itemize resources added to shared stock and newly unlocked crew gear. A crystal lock cost is shown separately from gross rewards, so a cache granting 8 crystal with a 2-crystal lock correctly increases the stockpile by 6.
- Shared treasure receipts reach every connected crew member; nearby harvesting feedback reaches observers within 30 metres. Restoring a snapshot does not replay old rewards.

## Enemy health

Damaged, living enemies have a small bar above their heads. Full-health enemies remain uncluttered. Ordinary bars are visible within 40 metres; dragons and the Colossus use wider bars within 90 metres. Solid-world occlusion hides bars behind walls. The overlay is capped at 80 visible enemy bars and hides while menus are open.

## Compatibility

Everyone in a session must use Fort 16. Existing Fort 14 and 15 saves load; saving still uses the Fort 15 format (schema 2). Network hosting, resource yields, equipment costs and combat balance are unchanged in this pass. Keep earlier version folders and save backups if you want to roll back.
