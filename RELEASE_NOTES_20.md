# Fort 20 — Recovery and clearer upstairs building

- Press **Esc → I'm stuck / move to safe ground** if you cannot escape an obstacle. If a build preview is active, Esc cancels it first; press again for the menu.
- Recovery checks nearby floor, slope, headroom, other players and enemies. It prefers your current storey, with checked hearth-area ground as a fallback. If no safe location exists, it asks for space to be cleared rather than moving you into another obstacle.
- The host handles recovery for every player. It keeps health, inventory and equipment, grants no healing or new invulnerability, works while downed, and releases a mounted ballista. There is a 15-second cooldown.
- A dwarf attempting to move while stationary and embedded in solid scenery can request automatic recovery. Simply walking into a wall does not trigger it.
- Smoothed the campfire's collision mound, removed collision from decorative hearth-upgrade runestones, and added short swept steps over low ledges. Solid world obstacles remain solid.
- Upstairs options show completed ground-wing progress instead of an unexplained “Blocked.” The placement button now has an adjacent requirement explanation that updates when you choose a different direction or room type.

## How to unlock upstairs

1. Finish four ground-floor wings by supplying their signs and holding E until construction completes. The grand stairwell counts as one; the starting keep does not. Unfinished blueprints do not count.
2. At a finished grand stairwell's sign, press K and select Upstairs. A courtyard is available at hearth tier 1; some other room purposes require higher hearth tiers.
3. Place the blueprint, then supply and build at its sign near the foot of the lower stairs.
4. Expand sideways from completed upper rooms. Every new upper room requires a completed room directly underneath it. Additional storeys require stairwells and sufficient hearth upgrades.

Trees and loose rocks are not an upstairs progression requirement. Existing automatic castle clearance and defense salvage rules are unchanged. Existing Fort 15–19 saves remain compatible. Multiplayer peers must all use Fort 20.
